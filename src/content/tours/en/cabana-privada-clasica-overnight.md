This is the entry point to having San Blas to yourself at night. A cabin on the sand with a door that closes, for couples and small groups who want their own space and would rather spend the difference on another night than on a nicer bed.

## What "simple" actually means here

Thatched roof, basic furnishings, and a view that does all the work. No air conditioning, no grid electricity, and bathrooms that are basic and usually shared — that's true of every category on these islands, and the Classic cabin is the version that doesn't pretend otherwise. What you're paying for is one thing: the space is yours.

Cabins are booked by **type, not by island**. Most San Blas islands run near-identical cabins within each category, so the exact island is confirmed closer to your date based on availability — that's how the operator keeps a bed open for you on a date it can actually deliver.

## Classic or Deluxe?

One difference, and it's worth being blunt about it: both are private cabins on the beach, and the [Deluxe](/tours/cabana-privada-deluxe-overnight) buys you a better-finished, more comfortable version of the same night. Nothing else in the stay changes — same island rhythm, same meals, same island tour.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is the bathroom private?

Usually not. The cabin is yours; the bathroom facilities on most islands are shared and basic. If a private bathroom is a requirement rather than a preference, tell us when you write and we'll say honestly what's available on your dates.

### Is there electricity or a fan?

Don't plan around it. These islands are off-grid, so power is limited and intermittent where it exists at all. The upside is the reason people come: no hum, no light, and a night sky that most travellers have never seen properly.

### Can I choose which island I stay on?

Not at booking. You choose the cabin type, and the island is confirmed closer to the date. If a specific island matters to you — say you want [Isla Diablo](/islands/isla-diablo) — ask before you pay so we can tell you whether it's realistic for your dates.

### How many people fit in one cabin?

It's sized for couples and small groups rather than large ones. Give us your exact group when you write and we'll confirm the capacity for that cabin type instead of guessing.

### What does the second day look like?

Breakfast, then the boat tour to two more islands with a stop at the Natural Pool, back to the island to check out, and the return to Panama City around midday. Longer stays add a free day in the middle.

### Can I stay on the beach later on my last morning?

Yes — late check-out is available for a small per-person fee, and it's the cheapest upgrade in the whole stay. Without it, check-out is early in the morning.
