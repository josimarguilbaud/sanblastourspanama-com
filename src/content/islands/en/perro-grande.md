The name means "big dog", and within the Isla Perro group the "big" part is the useful information. Most San Blas cays are tiny — a ring of sand you can cross in a minute. Perro Grande has genuine width to its beaches, which changes what a day here feels like more than any single attraction would.

## Room to spread out

On a small cay, everyone off the boat ends up on the same twenty metres of sand. Here they do not. That matters most on a busy day in the dry season, when several boats are out at once: the difference between a beach you share and a beach that feels like yours is mostly a question of metres, and Perro Grande has them.

## Water for swimming, not for chasing fish

The water is calm and consistently turquoise, which makes it excellent for floating and unremarkable for snorkeling. This is a place to swim slowly and get out when you feel like it, not a place to hunt for reef life — for that, the shipwreck at Perro Chico is a short ride away and does the job far better. Being clear about which of the two you want is the single most useful thing you can tell whoever plans your route.

## Staying the night

Perro Grande is one of the inhabited islands with simple beachfront cabins for travellers on a multi-day package. San Blas overnight stays are booked by **cabin type** rather than by island — shared dorm, private classic, private deluxe, camping under the stars, or an overwater cabin — and the exact island is confirmed closer to the date based on availability. Stays run one to three nights, and cabin availability needs at least three days' notice.

## What a night includes, and what it does not

Every cabin type includes round-trip 4x4 transport from Panama City, the boat transfers, the cabin itself, breakfast, lunch and dinner, a tour to two more islands, a stop at the Natural Pool and a Guna guide. It does not include drinks and snacks, alcohol, the comarca entry tax, snorkel gear, or personal items like towels and soap. Late check-out, until mid-afternoon rather than the morning boat, is available for a small per-person fee.

## Expect to be offline

There is no wifi on any island in San Blas, and cell signal is inconsistent — some islands catch a usable signal, most do not. On a wide, quiet beach with nothing to refresh, that tends to be the part people describe afterwards as the point of the trip rather than its inconvenience.

## Good to know

- **Best for:** travellers who want space, easy swimming and a night on the island rather than a rushed return.
- **Facilities:** basic beachfront cabins for overnight guests; shade shelters for day visitors.
- **Route note:** as everywhere in San Blas, which islands a given tour visits depends on the day's conditions and availability.

## Frequently asked questions

### Are Perro Grande and Perro Chico the same island?
No, though they belong to the same group and sit a short boat ride apart. Perro Chico is a small uninhabited cay known for the shipwreck snorkel; Perro Grande is larger, inhabited, and known for wide beaches and calm swimming water. People routinely book one meaning the other.

### Can I book a night specifically on Perro Grande?
Not by name. Overnight stays are sold by cabin type, and the island is assigned closer to your date according to availability — most San Blas islands offer near-identical cabins within each category. If a particular island matters to you, say so when you book and it can be taken into account, but it cannot be guaranteed.

### Is Perro Grande good for snorkeling?
It is not the island's strength. The water is calm and clear but the draw here is swimming and space, not reef life. The best snorkeling in the same group is the shipwreck at Perro Chico, and the best in the archipelago is the reef at Cayos Holandeses, which has its own dedicated tour.

### What does an overnight stay include?
Round-trip 4x4 transport from your Panama City accommodation, boat transfers, your cabin, all three meals, a tour to two additional islands, the Natural Pool and a local Guna guide. Drinks, alcohol, snorkel gear, towels and the comarca entry tax are not included and are paid separately.

### Is there wifi or phone signal on Perro Grande?
There is no wifi on any San Blas island. Cell signal across the archipelago is inconsistent and cannot be relied on, so tell anyone who might worry that you will be unreachable, and download anything you need before the 4x4 leaves Panama City.
