San Blas cooking runs on fresh fish and seafood, and across most of the archipelago plant-based options are thin. Skip the protein on a typical island and what you get is a larger portion of the sides. Isla Franklin is the one clear exception worth knowing about, and for some travellers it decides the trip.

## What eating on an island normally looks like

Meals on an overnight stay are cooked by the family hosting you and served at fixed times — a traditional Guna lunch around 12:30 PM and dinner around 6:00 PM on the day you arrive, breakfast at 7:00 AM the next morning. There is no menu to order from and no shop to top up at. Whatever the kitchen has is what there is, which is exactly why a dietary requirement has to be arranged before you leave Panama City rather than mentioned at the table.

## What makes Isla Franklin different

Isla Franklin is specifically known for offering genuine **vegan meals** to guests staying overnight — not a plate with the fish removed, but food planned that way. In a region where plant-based travellers usually have to work around what is available, that is a real point of difference, and it is the reason this island shows up in conversations that have nothing to do with beaches.

## How to actually ask for it

Here is the part that trips people up: San Blas overnight stays are booked by **cabin type**, not by island. You choose a shared dorm, a private classic or deluxe cabin, camping under the stars or an overwater cabin, and the island is confirmed closer to your date based on availability. So you cannot simply book "Isla Franklin". What you do instead is state your dietary needs when you book, so they can be factored into which island and which kitchen you are placed with. Cabin availability needs at least three days' notice.

## What is included at mealtimes, and what is not

Every cabin type includes breakfast, lunch and dinner. Drinks and snacks are not included, and neither is alcohol. There are no shops on the islands beyond basic snacks and no ATMs anywhere in Guna Yala, so bring cash and, if you want them, your own extras — a small cooler with snacks or drinks is allowed, unlike full-size ice boxes, which do not fit the boats.

## The rest of the stay

Food aside, the rhythm is the standard San Blas overnight: beach time, snorkeling, the island tour on the morning of Day 2, and an evening with the Guna family hosting you. There is no wifi on any island, and cell signal is unreliable, so plan on the evening being genuinely offline.

## Good to know

- **Best for:** vegan and plant-based travellers doing an overnight stay.
- **Booking note:** state dietary needs at the time of booking, not on arrival — the kitchen has no way to improvise on an island with no shop.
- **Beyond the food:** expect the ordinary rhythm of an island night, not a specialist retreat.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I book Isla Franklin by name?
Not directly. Overnight stays are sold by cabin type and the specific island is confirmed closer to your date according to availability. If Isla Franklin's food options are the reason you are asking, say so when you book: the requirement can be taken into account even though the island itself cannot be guaranteed.

### Is there really vegan food in San Blas?
On most islands, not in any planned sense — the cooking is built around fresh fish and seafood, and removing it leaves you with sides. Isla Franklin is the exception the archipelago is known for, with genuine vegan meals prepared for overnight guests rather than improvised on the day.

### What do meals look like on an overnight stay?
Three meals a day, cooked and served by the Guna family hosting you at set times: lunch around midday, dinner in the early evening, breakfast at 7:00 AM before the Day 2 island tour. It is home cooking on an island without a shop, not a restaurant with a menu.

### How far in advance do I need to declare a dietary requirement?
As early as you can, and in any case when you book rather than on arrival. Cabin availability itself needs a minimum of three days' notice, and a kitchen on an island with no shop and no daily delivery cannot solve a requirement it hears about for the first time at lunch.

### Are drinks included with meals?
No. All three meals are included in every cabin type, but drinks, snacks and alcohol are not, and they are paid for separately in cash. You are allowed to bring a small cooler with your own snacks and drinks if you would rather not buy on the island.
