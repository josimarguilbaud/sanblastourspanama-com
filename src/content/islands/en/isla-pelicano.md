Isla Pelícano earned a very specific kind of fame: it is one of the filming locations used for Netflix's *Money Heist* (*La Casa de Papel*), and some travellers plan an entire San Blas trip around standing on it. Take the show away and it is still a striking island — set well out toward the Cayos Holandeses, with clearer water and far fewer boats than the cays near Carti.

## The name it had first

Before it was a filming location it was **Gorgidub**, its Guna name, and that is what it is called by the people whose territory it sits in. Worth knowing if you ask a guide about it: "the Money Heist island" gets you a smile, "Gorgidub" gets you a conversation.

## Why so few boats reach it

Distance. Pelícano sits in the Cayos Holandeses sector, further from the mainland embarkation point than the islands a standard day tour can comfortably fit in. Boat time is the scarce resource on a San Blas day, and spending it on this crossing means giving up stops elsewhere — which is exactly why the water here is clearer and the beach emptier.

## Getting there: the private tour

Isla Pelícano belongs to the Super VIP Private itinerary rather than the shared day tours. That tour gives your group a private 4x4 and a private boat, with an itinerary you help decide within what the weather and the timing allow, so an island this far out becomes a reasonable ask rather than an impossible one. Lunch with a drink, life jackets and a dedicated Guna guide are included; the comarca entry tax is paid separately, in cash.

## What is actually on the island

Very little, which is the point. This is a scenic and snorkeling stop, not an island with infrastructure — no cabins, no shop, no facilities. There is nothing built here around the series either: what you are looking at is the island itself, the same one that appeared on screen, and nothing has been added for visitors since.

## Good to know

- **Best for:** fans of the series, photographers, and anyone on a private itinerary with real boat time built into the day.
- **Facilities:** minimal — bring water, biodegradable sunscreen and anything else you will want.
- **Snorkeling:** the water out here is noticeably clearer than around the islands closer to Carti, and it sees a fraction of the traffic.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is this really the island from Money Heist?
Yes — Isla Pelícano is one of the San Blas filming locations used for Netflix's *Money Heist* (*La Casa de Papel*). What you will find is the island as it is: there is no set, no exhibit and no infrastructure built around the show, just the beach and the water that appeared on screen.

### What is the island's Guna name?
Gorgidub. San Blas is Guna Yala, an autonomous comarca, and many islands carry a Guna name alongside the Spanish one that shows up on tour listings. Using it is a small courtesy that tends to be noticed.

### Which tour reaches Isla Pelícano?
The Super VIP Private day tour. Because the island sits far out toward the Cayos Holandeses, it does not fit the fixed route of a shared budget day trip; a private boat with a flexible itinerary is what makes it reachable within a single day.

### How is Isla Pelícano priced compared with a shared tour?
The Super VIP Private tour is priced per group rather than per person, so the rate does not change between a couple and a full group up to the maximum size. For a larger group the per-person arithmetic often lands closer to a shared tour than people expect — worth comparing before assuming it is out of reach.

### How does Isla Pelícano relate to the Cayos Holandeses?
It sits in the same sector, out beyond the busier cays. The difference is what each is for: Cayos Holandeses is a reef, visited on its own early-departure tour for the quality of the coral, while Pelícano is an island — a beach, a photograph and clear water, on a private itinerary you help shape.
