San Blas has no closed season. Boats leave the Carti embarkation point every day of the year, the water stays warm through all twelve months, and there is no month you should strike off the calendar. What does change is light, crowd level and how often you get wet from above — and choosing between those trade-offs is really what "best time" means here.

## Dry season: December to April

This is the stretch most travelers picture. Skies clear, rain becomes the exception rather than the rhythm of the day, and the Caribbean light that makes the water read turquoise instead of grey is at its most reliable. If your trip has one non-negotiable photograph in it, these are the months that deliver it most consistently.

The trade-off is company. Dry season is high season across Panama, boats fill earlier, and popular cays see more hulls pulled up on the sand. If you are set on a specific date — an anniversary, the one free Saturday in your trip — this is the half of the year where booking ahead genuinely matters.

## Green season: May to November

Rain here rarely behaves the way northern visitors expect. Instead of a grey day that never lifts, the pattern is a hard twenty-minute downpour followed by sun, often before you have finished drying off. Islands look their greenest, the sea is the same bath-warm temperature it is in February, and the whole archipelago is markedly quieter.

For travelers who care more about having a cay to themselves than about a perfect sky, these months are arguably the better buy. You accept the chance of a shower over lunch; you get emptier beaches in return.

## What actually changes between the two

- **Light for photography** — noticeably more dependable December to April.
- **Boat traffic on the popular cays** — heaviest in dry season, thinnest around the middle of the year.
- **Booking pressure** — dry-season dates disappear first, especially weekends and holiday periods.
- **Water temperature and island greenery** — effectively unchanged all year.
- **Departure times** — identical in both seasons: pickup runs 5:00–5:45 AM, and around 4:30 AM for the longer Dutch Cays route.

## Planning around the weather rather than fearing it

Because a day tour is a fixed 14-hour block, a single rainy morning is not a lost holiday — it is a wet hour on a boat between stops. Travelers with several days in Panama often keep their San Blas date flexible for exactly that reason, and simply pick the better-looking of two mornings. If conditions are genuinely unsafe to sail, the operator reschedules or refunds in full; ordinary tropical rain does not stop anything.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the best month to visit San Blas?

If clear skies are your priority, aim for the heart of the dry season, roughly January to March. If you would rather have quieter islands and do not mind a passing shower, any month in the middle of the green season works just as well — the water and the greenery are unchanged.

### Does it rain all day during the rainy season?

Rarely. The usual pattern in San Blas is a short, heavy burst — often twenty minutes or so — followed by sun. All-day rain happens, but it is not the norm, and an itinerary is built around island stops rather than a fixed outdoor schedule.

### Do tours run in the rainy season?

Yes. Tours operate every day of the year, in both seasons, with the same pickup times and the same itineraries. Nothing about the route changes between May and November.

### Will my tour be cancelled if the weather is bad?

Only if conditions are genuinely unsafe for the boat crossing. In that case the operator reschedules your date or refunds the trip in full — safety takes priority over the schedule. Ordinary rain is not grounds for cancellation on either side.

### When is San Blas most crowded?

The dry season, December through April, and particularly around public holidays and weekends. Even then "crowded" is relative: it means several boats sharing a cay, not a resort beach.

### How far ahead should I book?

For dry-season dates, as early as you can fix your plans. Overnight cabin stays require a minimum of three days' advance booking at any time of year in order to confirm availability, and premium options such as the overwater cabin have the tightest availability of all.

### Is the sea rough at certain times of year?

Conditions vary day to day rather than season to season, and the crossing from Carti is short. The captains are local Guna boatmen who read the water every morning; if a route is not safe on a given day, they do not run it.
