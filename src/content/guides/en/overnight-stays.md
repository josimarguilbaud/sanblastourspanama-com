A day tour shows you San Blas. Staying the night lets you be in it: the same stretch of sand at sunset and again at sunrise, a sky with no light pollution to spoil it, and an evening that belongs to the island rather than to the return convoy. Stays run **1 to 3 nights maximum**, and they are booked by **cabin type** rather than by a named island.

## The five cabin types

- **Hostal Isleño** — a shared dormitory, the most affordable way to wake up on a San Blas island and the natural pick for solo travelers and backpackers.
- **Cabaña Privada Clásica** — a simple private cabin on the beach, for couples or small groups who want their own door without paying for a premium setting.
- **Cabaña Privada Deluxe** — the same privacy and the same beachfront, with a step up in comfort and finish.
- **Camping Bajo las Estrellas** — camping under an open sky, built around the fact that Guna Yala has essentially no light pollution.
- **Cabaña Overwater** — the premium option, a private cabin built out over the water itself, and the one with the tightest availability.

Rates for each, including additional nights and the deposit, are confirmed by a real person over WhatsApp against your dates, because availability and pricing move.

## Why stays are booked by cabin type, not by island

Across San Blas, cabins within the same category are near-identical from one island to the next, so the booking fixes the standard of accommodation and the exact island is confirmed closer to your date according to availability. If something about a specific island genuinely matters to you — vegan cooking, for example, which Isla Franklin is known for — say so when you book so it can be factored in.

## What every stay includes

- Round-trip 4x4 transport from your Panama City accommodation
- Boat transfers
- Your chosen cabin
- Breakfast, lunch and dinner
- A tour to two additional islands
- A stop at the Natural Pool
- A local Guna guide

**Not included:** drinks and snacks, alcoholic beverages, the Guna Yala community tax (cash, paid separately), snorkel gear, and personal items such as towels and soap.

## A 2-day, 1-night itinerary

- **Day 1** — 5:00–5:45 AM pickup in Panama City, 8:30 AM arrival at Carti, 9:30 AM arrival at the island and check-in, 12:30 PM traditional Guna lunch, free afternoon, 6:00 PM dinner.
- **Day 2** — 7:00 AM breakfast, 7:45 AM island tour taking in the Limones Cays and the shipwreck snorkel, 8:30 AM the Natural Pool, 9:30 AM back to the island and check-out, 10:00 AM boat to Carti, arriving in Panama City around 1:00–1:30 PM.

Three- and four-day stays follow the same rhythm with an unstructured day in the middle — the natural slot for the optional three-island add-on tour.

## Booking rules worth knowing

- **Minimum three days' advance booking** is required to confirm cabin availability.
- **Late check-out** — until roughly 3:00–3:30 PM instead of the standard 8:30 AM departure — is available for a small per-person fee, and is the single upgrade most guests wish they had taken.
- **Children under 4 stay free**, and the current child rate for each cabin type is confirmed when you book.

## Frequently asked questions

### How many nights can I stay in San Blas?

Between one and three. Three nights is the maximum per booking, which makes a four-day, three-night trip the longest standard stay available.

### Which island will I be sleeping on?

That is confirmed closer to your travel date, based on availability. Stays are sold by cabin type because cabins in the same category are essentially equivalent across islands. If a particular island matters to you, raise it at the time of booking.

### What does an overnight stay include?

Round-trip 4x4 transport from Panama City, boat transfers, your cabin, all three meals a day, a tour to two additional islands, a stop at the Natural Pool and a Guna guide. Drinks, alcohol, snorkel gear, towels and the community tax are separate.

### Is there electricity, wifi or hot water?

There is no wifi on any island, and cell signal is inconsistent — some islands have a usable one, most do not. Most islands have no mains electricity, and cabins are rustic by design: thatched roofs, often shared bathrooms, no air conditioning.

### How far in advance do I need to book?

At least three days, at any time of year, so that cabin availability can be confirmed. For the overwater cabin, which has the least inventory, considerably earlier is wise.

### Can I check out later than 8:30 AM?

Yes. Late check-out until around 3:00–3:30 PM is offered for a small per-person fee, which buys you most of a final beach day instead of an early boat back.

### Do children pay for an overnight stay?

Children under 4 stay free. Above that age a child rate applies, and it is confirmed for your chosen cabin type at the time of booking.
